# Product Vision

> Version 1.0  
> Last Updated: 2026-07-23

---

# 1. Vision

## Mission

Enable every traveler to preserve meaningful memories effortlessly through AI.

RoRo minimizes the effort required to document a trip while maximizing the emotional value users gain after returning home.

---

## Vision Statement

RoRo is not a travel planner.

RoRo is a travel memory platform.

Instead of helping people prepare for travel, RoRo helps people remember travel.

---

# 2. Why RoRo Exists

## Current Problems

Today's travelers experience several recurring pain points.

### Before Travel

- Trip planning is fragmented across spreadsheets, maps, notes, and messaging apps.
- Plans are rarely updated during the trip.

### During Travel

- Users spend time taking photos but rarely organize them.
- Expenses are recorded separately or forgotten.
- Writing a travel diary interrupts the travel experience.

### After Travel

- Thousands of photos remain unsorted.
- Receipts are discarded.
- Memories fade over time.
- Most trips leave no structured record.

---

# 3. Product Goal

RoRo automatically transforms travel activities into meaningful memories.

Users should only need to:

- Travel
- Take photos
- Save receipts

Everything else should be assisted by AI.

---

# 4. Product Principles

## AI Assists, Humans Decide

AI generates suggestions.

Users always have final approval.

---

## Capture Moments, Not Tasks

Travel should never feel like data entry.

Interactions should require minimal effort.

---

## Family First

The first version of RoRo is optimized for family travel.

Future versions may expand to solo travelers, couples, and group travel.

---

## Privacy by Default

Travel memories belong to the traveler.

Sharing is always optional.

---

## Simplicity Wins

If a feature increases complexity without improving the travel experience, it should not be included in the MVP.

---

# 5. Target Users

## Primary

Families traveling together.

Characteristics:

- Shared memories
- Many photos
- Expense tracking
- Children
- Multiple destinations

---

## Secondary

Couples

---

## Future

- Solo Travelers
- Friends
- Business Travelers

---

# 6. Success Metrics

## MVP Success

A successful MVP means:

- Users complete an entire trip using RoRo.
- AI generates a diary every day.
- Expenses are tracked throughout the trip.
- A highlight video is generated after the trip.
- Users are willing to use RoRo again.

---

## Product KPIs

### Activation

- Trip Created

### Engagement

- Moments Created per Day

### Retention

- Second Trip Created

### AI Adoption

- AI Diary Acceptance Rate

### Satisfaction

- User Rating

---

# 7. Non-Goals (MVP)

The following features are intentionally excluded from the MVP.

- Social Network
- Public Community
- Destination Recommendation
- AI Chatbot
- Offline Mode
- Multi-language Support
- Gamification

---

# 8. Long-term Vision

RoRo aims to become the personal memory operating system for travel.

Future capabilities include:

- Shared family timelines
- AI storytelling
- Personalized travel books
- Memory search using natural language
- Annual travel recap
- Smart recommendations based on past experiences

---

# 9. Product North Star
